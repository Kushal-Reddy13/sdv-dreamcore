What is the Jetson AGX Orin?

The Jetson AGX Orin Developer Kit is a small but very powerful computer built for artificial intelligence, robotics and vehicle applications. It runs Ubuntu Linux. In our DreamCore project it is the main CPU: it runs the DreamCore software, shows Ubuntu and the HMI on the instrument cluster, receives commands from the online digital.auto Playground through Wi-Fi and passes them over Ethernet to the Raspberry Pi (zonal CPU).
Car analogy: The Jetson is the central ECU of the car. The Ethernet cable is the wiring harness, the Raspberry Pi is a zonal control unit, the relay is the power stage and the Playground is the engineer's office that sends commands.

What is the Raspberry Pi 4?

The Raspberry Pi 4 Model B is a credit-card-sized computer. It runs Raspberry Pi OS (Linux) from a microSD card and has a 40-pin GPIO header that can switch electrical signals. In our DreamCore project it is the zonal CPU: it receives commands from the Jetson (main CPU) over Ethernet and switches a relay module with a GPIO pin. The relay then switches the beam light (real head lamp) that is powered by a 12 V battery.
Car analogy: The Raspberry Pi is a zonal control unit placed near the actuators. The GPIO pin is its control wire, the relay is the power stage and the beam light is the actuator (the headlight).

What is the Ethernet switch?

An Ethernet switch is a small box that connects several network cables together, so all connected devices can talk to each other. Our BrosTrend switch is unmanaged: there is nothing to configure - plug in the cables and it works. In our DreamCore project it connects the Jetson (192.168.56.48) and the Raspberry Pi (192.168.56.50) in a small local network. It does not provide internet - the Jetson gets internet through Wi-Fi.
Car analogy: The switch is the junction box / distributor of the wiring harness - like a multi-socket connector that links several control units on the same bus. It only forwards messages; it does not decide anything itself.

What is the relay module?

A relay is an electrically operated switch. A small control signal (3.3 V from a GPIO pin) energises a coil; the coil pulls a metal contact and closes a separate circuit. The relay module already contains everything needed to drive the coil safely (optocoupler, transistor, flyback diode, LEDs). In our project each controller switches one relay, and the relay switches a beam light (head lamp) powered by a 12 V battery.
Car analogy: Like the headlight relay in a car: the light switch only sends a small signal, the relay switches the big lamp current from the battery.

MODULE 8 · QUICK QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Quick Q&A

Short questions about the whole bench. Think of your answer first, then open the answer to check it.

Answer each question in your head or aloud, open Show answer to check it, then tick I know this answer. When all questions are ticked, you can mark the module complete.
Question 01

What is the job of the Jetson AGX Orin on our bench?

Show answer

It is the main computer (central ECU). It runs DreamCore, the KUKSA Databroker and the HMI, and it reaches the digital.auto Playground through Wi-Fi.

Question 02

What is the job of the Raspberry Pi 4?

Show answer

It is the zonal controller (zonal ECU). It gets commands from the Jetson over Ethernet and switches the relay with a GPIO pin (pin 11 = GPIO17).

Question 03

Why do we need the Ethernet switch?

Show answer

It connects the Jetson (192.168.56.48) and the Raspberry Pi (192.168.56.50) in one local network. It gives no internet – the internet comes from the Jetson Wi-Fi.

Question 04

What is the NXP GoldBox used for?

Show answer

It is a vehicle-networking platform from NXP. In DreamCore it is the second node of the cluster (worker vip). It is set up in the Bench Setup Course.

Question 05

Why can a GPIO pin not switch the beam light directly?

Show answer

A GPIO pin only gives 3.3 V and a very small current. The beam light needs 12 V from the battery. So the GPIO pin controls the relay, and the relay switches the beam light.

Question 06

Which relay contacts do we use for the beam light, and why?

Show answer

COM and NO. Battery + goes to COM, NO goes to the beam light +. NO is open while the relay is OFF, so the beam light is only ON when the relay is switched ON.

Question 07

Which three wires connect the controller to the relay?

Show answer

5 V → VCC, GND → GND and the GPIO pin → IN (Jetson pin 13, Raspberry Pi pin 11).

Question 08

What is the golden rule when you build the bench?

Show answer

All cables first – the power adapters and the 12 V battery are always connected last.
